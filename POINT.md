# The point of ai_workflow

The files here are rules, skills, and scripts for AI assistants: Claude Code,
Codex, the Claude app (via plugins), and OpenCode. The assistants read the
rules and skills. Two groups of people read what the assistants then write. The
first is Jake. The second is the coauthors, students, and colleagues who
work with and use these files.

An AI assistant that has loaded these files should write code, mathematics, and
prose that Jake can read without stopping to ask what a sentence means or why
he is reading it. Jake should be able to explain what he read to someone else in
his own words. Prose drafted for him should sound like him.

The work in this repository is meant to enable Jake to spend more of his time
on the research, math, coding, writing, and thinking and less of it on
correcting the AI assistant.

The files do not claim that Jake's rules suit other readers. The description
of how Jake reads, which the first-reader skill uses, models only him and is
kept out of this public repository.
